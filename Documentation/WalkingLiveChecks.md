# Walking Live checks

## Approach timing

Choose Walking, set Spoken Intersections to One, and start Live before approaching a mapped intersection. Walk at your usual pace, then repeat at a slower and a brisker pace. Note the first announcement's approximate distance from the intersection and whether the whole report finishes before you reach it.

The target is about 15 seconds of walking, within 12 to 30 meters of the mapped junction. At 1.4 meters per second, that is about 21 meters. Location updates arrive at intervals, so the actual announcement can occur closer. Mapped junction positions do not establish the curb or the exact end of a crossing.

## Waiting and proceeding

After an announcement, wait before the intersection. Moving the phone or small location shifts should not repeat that report. Continue across and along the next block. The next automatic report should wait until you have moved beyond the current mapped junction and are approaching the next one.

Repeat on a street with closely spaced intersections, a divided or staggered junction, and a corner where you turn. Listen for repeated street names, an announcement while still crossing, or an intersection skipped after turning.

## Multiple intersections and speech

Set Spoken Intersections to Two or Three. Intersections already included in a report should not be repeated immediately when they become the nearest upcoming junction. Use your usual speech rate and confirm that an existing Live report finishes before another starts.

## Location and loading recovery

Try an area where location reception varies, and a first request that needs a map download. Walking should wait when location uncertainty exceeds 20 meters. A delayed download should use the newest accepted position, without announcing an intersection already passed.

Repeat with the phone locked and with another app open. Stop and restart Live, and verify that a new session can announce the current approach again. Switch between Walking and Driving using the existing actions and verify that each mode resumes its own timing.

## Reporting results

Include the mode, Spoken Intersections count, speech rate, approximate walking pace, intersection names, and whether the phone was locked. For early, missing, or repeated speech, describe where you were for each announcement and whether you were waiting, crossing, turning, or continuing straight.

Automated route and coordinator tests verify the decision rules. Physical walking, background operation, speech routes, and location accuracy require device testing.
